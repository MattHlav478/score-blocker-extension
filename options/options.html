Master switch

Detection rules

Match day

The aggressive layers. They run only while the Match Day switch in the popup is on — with it off, nothing here has any effect.

Spoiler words

One per line or comma-separated. A block containing any of these is hidden whole while Match Day is on. Deliberately broad — false positives cost one click.

Team names

One per line or comma-separated. A score next to any of these is masked even with no keyword present.

Sports keywords

One per line or comma-separated. Used by the "score pair near a keyword" rule.

Extra sites

Google and YouTube are built in. Add other sites here — Chrome will ask for permission, and the scanner is registered for them without touching the manifest.
