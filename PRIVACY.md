# Score Blocker — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 29 August 2026_

Score Blocker does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

## What it accesses

On the sites you have granted it access to, the extension reads the text of the page in order to
identify sports scores and spoiler wording, and applies a blur to the parts that match. This happens
entirely in your browser, as the page is displayed. Page content is never stored, logged, or sent
anywhere.

## What it stores

Only your own settings: whether the extension is on, whether Match Day mode is on, which detection
rules are active, your team names, keywords and spoiler words, and any sites you have added.

These are held in Chrome's extension storage. If you are signed in to Chrome, Chrome synchronises
them to your Google account so they follow you between your own devices. That synchronisation is
performed by Chrome itself; the developer of this extension has no access to it.

## What it does not do

The extension makes no network requests. It contains no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party
code or services. It does not read your browsing history. It does not sell or share any data,
because it does not collect any.

## Permissions

- **Storage** — to save the settings described above.
- **Scripting** — to inject the extension's own bundled stylesheets and content script, and to
  remove them again when a setting is switched off.
- **google.com and youtube.com** — the two sites the extension is built to work on.
- **Other sites (optional)** — only ever granted one site at a time, by you, through Chrome's own
  permission prompt, and revoked when you remove the site.
